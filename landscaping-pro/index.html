COMMON QUESTIONS

What to know.

Can I try the form?

Yes. Enter fictional details to see a sample inquiry summary. The form runs in your browser and does not send or store your information.

Is Fieldwork a real landscaping company?

No. Fieldwork is a fictional business created to demonstrate website design and interactions. Photography illustrates the concept and is not a record of completed client work.

Could this be adapted for a real business?

Yes. Branding, services, project photography, service areas, and contact details could be replaced with real business content. A working inquiry delivery service would also need to be connected.
