A FEW DETAILS

Good to know.

Are these real salon prices?

No. These are sample starting prices for a fictional studio. A real salon would confirm pricing after consultation, based on the service and time needed.

Does the form book an appointment?

No. It creates a preview in your browser. It does not send a request, reserve a time, or save your information.

What should I include in my request?

Tell us the service you are interested in, your preferred timing, and what you have in mind for your hair. A current photo and a reference look can help with a consultation.
